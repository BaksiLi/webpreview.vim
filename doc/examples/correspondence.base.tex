{\LARGE One missing edge\par}
\medskip
Write $R(x)$ for the worlds within one step of $x$.
Transitivity says that two steps never take you
somewhere a single step could not reach:
\[
  \bigcup_{y\in R(x)} R(y) \subseteq R(x).
\]
The inclusion holds at the initial world.

Suppose $Rab$ and $Rbc$ but not $Rac$.
If $p$ holds at exactly the worlds in $R(a)$,
then it holds one step from $a$ but fails
two steps away, at $c$. This refutes axiom~4.
